About Taniti

Taniti is a small tropical island in the Pacific (under 500 sq miles) with varied terrain: sandy and rocky beaches, lush rainforests, and a mountainous interior with a small active volcano.

Taniti has an indigenous population of about 20,000. Historically the economy focused on fishing and agriculture, but tourism has recently increased.
