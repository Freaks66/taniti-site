Lodging

From budget-friendly to upscale stays—Taniti has a wide variety of lodging.

Options

  • Inexpensive hostel
  • Family-owned hotels
  • Bed & breakfasts (growing)
  • One large four-star resort

Inspections & Regulations

All types of lodging are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.
