Transportation

Learn how to arrive and get around Taniti.

Arriving on the Island

  • Most visitors arrive by air.
  • Some arrive via a small cruise ship that docks in Yellow Leaf Bay one night per week.
  • The airport supports small jets and prop planes; expansion is in progress for larger jets.

Ground Transportation

  • Public buses in Taniti City: 5 a.m. – 11 p.m. daily
  • Private buses serve the rest of the island
  • Taxis available in Taniti City
  • Rental cars near the airport
  • Bikes + helmets for rent (helmets required by law)

Taniti City is fairly flat and walkable. Merriton Landing is easy to explore on foot.
